EvacReady

Stay prepared and respond faster during emergencies.

EvacReady connects residents, evacuation center staff, and local disaster response admins on one platform so communities can prepare for, respond to, and recover from emergencies together.

Purpose

EvacReady was built to close the gap between local disaster response offices and the residents they serve. During emergencies, timely alerts, accurate evacuee tracking, and clear communication save lives. EvacReady gives barangays and municipalities the tools to broadcast alerts, manage evacuation centers, and keep an accurate record of who has checked in and out, while giving residents a simple way to stay informed and request help.

The Problem We Solve

  • Delayed information: Residents often learn about evacuation orders too late or through unreliable channels.
  • Manual tracking: Evacuation centers rely on paper logs, making it hard to know who is safe in real time.
  • Disconnected response teams: Admins, staff, and residents lack a shared system to coordinate during a crisis.

EvacReady solves this with real-time alerts, digital check-in/check-out via QR codes, and role-based dashboards for admins, evacuation center staff, and residents.

How to Use EvacReady

  1. Create an account and complete your resident profile (name, phone number, and barangay).
  2. Watch for alerts under Alert Notification for evacuation orders and safety updates from your local disaster office.
  3. Check in at an evacuation center by presenting your Resident ID QR code to staff on arrival.
  4. Track your history anytime under Check-in History to see past and active evacuation records.
  5. Call for help instantly using the Emergency Call hotlines if you need immediate assistance.
